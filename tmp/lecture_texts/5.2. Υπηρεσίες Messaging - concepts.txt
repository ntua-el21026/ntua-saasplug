===== PAGE 31/47 =====
Kafka operation
Operation principles
Messages are available to consumers when they
■
have been committed
- "are available" = can be read
Kafka does not push
■
- the client has to ask for a message
Read does not destroy
■
- the message can be available after it has been read by
consumers (this can change, if needed)

===== PAGE 32/47 =====
Kafka operation (cont'd)
(some) History available
■
- Offline consumers can catch up, not "indefinitely"
- Consumers can re-consume from the (available) past
- This behaviour can be tuned
Delivery Guarantees
■
- Ordering of messages is maintained
- Ack at least once (per consumer) by default
- "At-most-once" and "exactly-once" can be implemented
